How it works

From your app to your next video.

  1. Knowledge Your app · from the listing and the site

    What it offers
    Sleep tracking with a smart alarm From the App Store listing
    What potential users care about
    “Will it wake me gently without waking my partner?” From the reviews
    How it sounds
    Calm, specific, no miracle promises From the website
    What it never says
    No medical claims Your rules

    01

    Understand your app.

    Start with your website or App Store listing. Videogo develops a working understanding of your product, its customers and the communication opportunities worth investigating. Add existing photos or footage when useful.

    02

    Turn ideas into finished videos.

    Videogo selects a relevant communication approach and produces the work. Each finished video is checked for quality, accuracy and consistency with your business. Request a correction without managing the editing process yourself.

  3. Learning What the results suggest next

    Published Smart alarm vs phone alarm

    Live 5 days on the channels you allowed. Watch time and saves are in; installs attributed where the store reports them.

    Learn what deserves amplification

    Publish through supported channels with the permissions you grant. Available results help determine whether to repeat a message, change its presentation, investigate a different angle or reconsider the underlying assumption.
