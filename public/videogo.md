## From your app to your next video

1. Understand your app. Start with your website or App Store listing.
   Videogo develops a working understanding of your product, its
   customers and the communication opportunities worth investigating.
   Add existing photos or footage when useful.
2. Turn ideas into finished videos. Videogo selects a relevant
   communication approach and produces the work. Each finished video is
   checked for quality, accuracy and consistency with your business.
   Request a correction without managing the editing process yourself.
3. Learn what deserves amplification. Publish through supported
   channels with the permissions you grant. Available results help
   determine whether to repeat a message, change its presentation,
   investigate a different angle or reconsider the underlying
   assumption.

## Pricing

A founding partnership for consumer-app growth. There is no public price
list; we define deliverables, volume, price, support, publication rights
and duration in a written offer.

- Single Consumer App: for one launched app with a recurring
  customer-acquisition problem. Messaging exploration, an agreed
  social-video production package, quality review and evidence-informed
  next decisions.
- App Portfolio Operator: for teams operating several consumer apps.
  Separate business context, authorized materials, deliverables and
  measurement for each app. Commercial scope agreed individually.

Paid campaign management, creator sourcing, bulk account creation and
guaranteed growth results are not included in the standard pilot. Access
to app analytics, advertising accounts and case-study rights requires
separate permission. Details: https://videogo.ai/pricing
